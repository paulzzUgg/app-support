App: Blank Sag
Developer: Eugene Harmon
Contact: eugene.harmon53@sina.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Blank Sag is an offline iOS tool for an optical shop. It stores lens blanks and polishing laps on the iPhone, computes sagitta and edge thickness, and picks the closest saved lap. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Photo library. The photo library is used only when you attach a reference photo to a blank. The app keeps that image on the device.

Data on device. Blanks, laps, and saved readings stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the report you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
