QSO Rose is an offline iOS tool for amateur radio operators. It turns ADIF contacts into a bearing rose and beam filter around your station grid. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Location. The app can read your location once when you ask for a station grid. It does not load online maps.

Files. The app can import an ADIF file when you choose Import. Files stay under your control.

Data on device. QSOs and station settings are saved on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, content you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
