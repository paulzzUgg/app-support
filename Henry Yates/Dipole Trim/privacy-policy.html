Dipole Trim is an offline iOS tool for amateur radio operators. You enter SWR readings, fit a resonance curve, and see how much to cut or add on each dipole leg. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Motion. The Angle page can use the motion sensors while open to measure a leg tilt. It does not keep continuous motion logs.

Data on device. Antennas, readings and cuts are saved on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, content you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
