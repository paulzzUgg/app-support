App: Sample Board

Effective: November 20, 2026

Sample Board is an offline iOS tool for CNC hobbyists. You rate a speed-and-feed sample board, see clean cells, and export a refined grid. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Files. The app can import a tool CSV and export G-code when you choose those actions. Files stay under your control.

Data on device. Boards, tools and ratings are saved on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, content you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
