App: Keyer Rhythm
Developer: Frank Nolan
Contact: frank.nolan28@163.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Keyer Rhythm is an offline iOS tool for amateur radio operators practicing Morse sending. It times key-downs from the screen, a sidetone mic, or a light sensor, clusters dits and dahs, and scores letter rhythm. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Microphone. The microphone is used only when you choose Mic keying. The app measures a volume envelope and does not keep audio recordings.

Camera. The camera is used only when you choose Light keying. The app reads brightness in a small center box and does not keep images.

Data on device. Drills and sessions are saved on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, content you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
