Privacy Policy

App: Wheel Pedal
Developer: Alice Vaughn
Contact: alice.vaughn81@163.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Wheel Pedal is an offline iOS tool for potters. With the phone lying flat on the wheel head, it reads how fast the wheel turns at each pedal mark and works out which mark to press for each throwing stage. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Motion data. While the Spin page is open, the app reads the gyroscope to measure the wheel's speed. Readings are turned into a speed per pedal mark and are not kept as raw motion data.

Data on device. Wheels, calibrations and stages are saved as files on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the pedal card you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
