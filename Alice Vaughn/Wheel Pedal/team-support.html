Wheel Pedal Support

Developer: Alice Vaughn
Contact: alice.vaughn81@163.com

Wheel Pedal calibrates a potter's wheel pedal. Lay the phone flat in the middle of the wheel head, screen up, press the pedal to one mark and wait until the ring turns solid. Then hold Hold to record. A damp cloth under the phone stops it from sliding.

A reading is steady when the last two seconds stay within 3 percent. Below 5 rpm nothing is recorded. At least three marks are needed before a pedal mark can be worked out for a stage.

On the home screen, hold a stage band and slide it to change its target speed; the pedal mark updates. Out of reach means the wheel never turns that fast. Belt slip names the mark that turned slower than the one below it. Drifted means the top speed moved more than 10 percent since the last calibration.

The last stage and the last wheel cannot be deleted. The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to alice.vaughn81@163.com if a reading never turns steady or a stage will not save.
