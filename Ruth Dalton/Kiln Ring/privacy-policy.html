Privacy Policy

App: Kiln Ring
Developer: Ruth Dalton
Contact: ruth.dalton48@163.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Kiln Ring is an offline iOS tool for potters. After a kiln unload, you tap each piece with a knuckle while holding the Listen control; the app measures how the sound decays and whether two close tones suggest a crack, then places the piece on a kiln map. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Microphone. The app uses the microphone only while you hold the Listen control. It measures short envelopes and spectra as numbers. It does not keep audio recordings.

Data on device. Firings, pieces, taps and forms are saved in a local database on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the kiln sheet you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
