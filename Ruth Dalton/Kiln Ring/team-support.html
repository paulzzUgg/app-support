App: Kiln Ring
Developer: Ruth Dalton

Kiln Ring helps you ring-test pottery after a kiln unload. Hold Listen, tap a piece, and read Rings, Short ring, Dull or Split tone. Drag pieces on the kiln map to see bad-piece clusters. Match pitch against a form baseline once three good pieces exist.

Tips. Keep the phone on a table 20–40 cm from the piece. Tap just below the rim. If the screen says Too noisy, wait for a quieter room. Microphone access is requested only for Listen; you can deny it and still browse saved firings.

Privacy. See the Privacy Policy linked from the App Store listing. No account is required.
