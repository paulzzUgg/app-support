Privacy Policy

App: Board Move
Developer: Harold Keller
Contact: harold.keller14@gmail.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Board Move is an offline iOS tool for woodworkers. It helps you plan when panels move through joinery and drying by month, with end-grain photos and measurement marks. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Camera. The app uses the camera only when you choose to photograph an end grain. Photos stay on your iPhone unless you share them.

Data on device. Projects, panels and measurements are saved on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, content you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
