Privacy Policy

App: Tap Tone
Developer: Alice Nolan
Contact: alice.nolan53@outlook.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Tap Tone is an offline iOS tool for luthiers and woodworkers. You tap a billet while holding the capture control; the app records the spectrum on a log-log plot and estimates thickness from the ringing. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Microphone. The app uses the microphone only while you hold the tap capture control. It measures short spectra as numbers and does not keep audio recordings.

Data on device. Billets, taps and pairings are saved on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, content you share leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
