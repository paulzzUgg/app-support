Glaze Unity Support

Developer: Frank Brooks
Contact: frank.brooks91@yahoo.com

Glaze Unity places a glaze recipe on the Stull chart. Drag a material bar on the home screen and the point moves; when it crosses a zone line the zone name changes. A recipe with no flux is not saved. The last recipe stays. A tile photo does not change the recipe.

Zone lines follow the published Stull chart and are an approximation. A fired test tile has the last word.

The app works offline. It does not ask you to sign in and it has no purchases.

To dismiss the keyboard, tap Done above the keyboard, or tap the empty page.

Write to frank.brooks91@yahoo.com if a recipe will not save or the zone does not change.
