Privacy Policy

App: Glaze Unity
Developer: Frank Brooks
Contact: frank.brooks91@yahoo.com
Effective: November 20, 2026

Glaze Unity is an offline iOS tool for potters. It turns a glaze recipe into a unity formula and places it on a Stull chart as Matte, Satin, Gloss or Underfired. It does not require an account and does not include in-app purchases.

Data on device. Recipes, material analyses, batch sizes and tile notes stay on your iPhone. The app does not upload them to our servers.

Photos. A tile photo is read only when you tap Photo on the tile page and pick one. The photo stays on the device and does not change the recipe.

Sharing. If you use the system share sheet, the formula you choose leaves the device under the rules of the destination you pick.

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13. The age rating is 4+.

Changes. We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
